# f06s1-cr-2: The constraint line reports filters the executor never applies

**Severity**: LOW — an invented reason for a zero, on plans that attach a filter to a
traversal step.
**Status**: Fixed
**Branch**: — (repo policy: commit on `master`, one finding per commit)
**Commit**: `<filled in after commit>`

## Evidence
Recorded as the reviewer stated it:

> `csharp/Services/AnswerReductionBuilder.cs:244-247` collects `Filters` from every plan step
> unconditionally. Execution only consumes filters for search steps:
> `csharp/Services/DirectoryPlanExecutor.cs:267-275` dispatches by operation,
> `csharp/Services/DirectoryPlanExecutor.cs:279-344` uses filters in `ExecuteSearchStep`, while
> `expand_members` and `lookup` at `csharp/Services/DirectoryPlanExecutor.cs:360-394` ignore
> `step.Filters`.

Verified before admitting: `ExecuteExpandMembersStep` traverses from the source step's records
straight to `ExpandGroupMembersAsync`, and `ExecuteLookupStep` reads `SourceAttribute` values
and calls `LookupAsync`. Neither reads `step.Filters` at all.

## Predicted observable failure
> If a model attaches a filter to an `expand_members` or `lookup` step and the final result is
> empty, the answer can say that records had to match that filter even though the executor
> never applied it. The operator sees a bogus constraint as the reason for zero rows.

## Approach
`AppliesItsFilters` gates collection to `search` steps, the only operation whose execution path
consumes the field. Projection filters are still reported — those *are* applied, in memory,
after the row step.

Deliberately **not** done: rejecting such a plan at validation, the reviewer's alternative
remedy. That is a real question but a different one, with its own blast radius — a plan the
engine currently tolerates would start failing. This slice is about explaining a zero
truthfully, so it reports what was applied and changes no execution behaviour.

## Guard proof
`FiltersOnAStepTheExecutorNeverFilters_AreNotReported` builds a two-step plan whose
`expand_members` step carries a `Department` filter and asserts the seed step's filter is named
while the ignored one is not. **Failed against the pre-fix builder**, passes after.
`pwsh -NoLogo -NoProfile -File scripts/verify.ps1` — 383 tests, 0 warnings, both published
smokes, audit clean.

## Coder dispute (if any)
None on the defect. The remedy is narrower than one of the two the reviewer offered, for the
reason given above.

## Known gaps
A filter attached to a traversal step is now silently absent from the explanation rather than
wrongly present. Whether the *plan* should be rejected for carrying a filter the engine will
ignore is a separate question, unaddressed here and worth its own decision.
