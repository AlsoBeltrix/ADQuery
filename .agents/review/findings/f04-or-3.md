## Predicted observable failure

For the extensionAttribute1 query the reduction that reaches Narrate is: total 47,388 and the top ten buckets. "26,612 values appear once" is unreachable from that. The model either fabricates the figure (violating its own prompt) or omits it and presents `Contractor` as the most common value — the misleading answer this whole plan exists to eliminate.

## What

The plan asserts `HeadlineResult` **is** the bounded reduction (`:90`, HEADLINE-D1 restatement) and simultaneously demands an answer that needs distribution shape `HeadlineResult` does not carry. Both cannot be true.

## Approach

Amend Slice 2 to compute a small, deterministic **distribution summary** alongside the headline when the plan is grouped: total rows, distinct-bucket count, singleton-bucket count, blank count, and the top-N buckets (case-folded per D4/Slice 1b). These are a fixed handful of scalars — well inside the DATA-D1 ceiling and the existing byte cap, and they carry no additional AD values beyond the buckets already permitted. Note in the HEADLINE-D1 constraint bullet that F04 sends headline **plus** this summary, so the plan stops claiming the headline alone suffices.
